I am a Ph.D. student at UT Austin, advised by Dr. Luis Sentis in the [Human Centered Robotics Lab](https://sites.utexas.edu/hcrl/). My research focuses on [Contact Morphology Design](/whatiscmd) for dexterous manipulation. I design geometry, compliance, transmission, and sensing at the contact interface to make physical interaction predictable, observable, and exploitable by the controller.
