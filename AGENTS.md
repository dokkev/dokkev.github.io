# Repository writing guidance

When writing or editing blog posts, project descriptions, research explanations,
documentation, or website copy, read and follow
[docs/writing/style-guide.md](docs/writing/style-guide.md).
It is the shared style authority for Korean and English prose across this repo.
Apply topic-specific guidance alongside it; use the shared guide for general
wording and structure, unless the user gives a more specific instruction.
Do not rewrite existing articles as a side effect of updating the guide.
