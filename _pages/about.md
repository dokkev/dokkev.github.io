I am a Ph.D. student at UT Austin, advised by Dr. Luis Sentis in the [Human Centered Robotics Lab](https://sites.utexas.edu/hcrl/). My research focuses on **contact morphology design** for robotic manipulation: designing the geometry, compliance, transmission, and sensing structures that shape how local contact deforms, transmits force, and is sensed. I aim to build contact interfaces whose mechanics are predictable and legible to the controller, providing a more trustworthy physical foundation for dexterous manipulation.
